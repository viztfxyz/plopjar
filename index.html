    How to play

    Takes a minute to learn.

    Grab any blob from the shelf and slide it along the top of the jar. Let go and it floats down gently.

    Pull down into the jar to throw. The further you pull, the harder it goes. Drop it back on the shelf to change your mind.

    Swap shelf blobs by dragging one onto another. You get one swap after every throw. The small blob at the end of the shelf is coming next.

    Matching numbers merge and double. Quick merges in a row build a combo. Matching neighbours on the shelf fuse on their own.

    Don’t overfill. If blobs stay above the dashed line for 3 seconds, the game ends.

    Power-ups. Merges charge the three dots at the bottom. Shake loosens a jammed jar. Pop removes one blob you tap.

    Rainbow blobs merge with the first blob they touch.

    Jar trail has 24 puzzle jars. Each has a goal and a limited number of throws. Finish in fewer throws for more stars.

    Chaos mode changes a rule every 25 seconds: low gravity, wind, bouncy walls and more.

    Collect every blob. Each number is a character. Bigger ones grow eyebrows, a sprout, a crown.

    Keyboard. Arrow keys aim, Space drops, 1 to 4 or Q and E pick a shelf blob, Esc pauses.

    About Plopjar

    A free merge puzzle game you play in your browser.

    Plopjar is a physics merge game. You fling soft, squishy number blobs into a glass jar. When two blobs with the same number touch, they merge into one blob with double the number. Chain merges together for combos, and keep the jar from filling past the line.

    Offline and private

    Plopjar works offline once installed and runs on phones, tablets and computers. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no cookies, and the game sends nothing anywhere. Scores, settings and progress live only in this browser on this device. Reset progress in Settings erases them.

    To install on iPhone or iPad, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

    Credits

    Game modes

    Play the Classic jar, squeeze into the narrow Slim jar, merge in sets of three in Threes, race to 1024 in Sprint, relax in Zen with no game over, or try Chaos, where a rule changes every 25 seconds: low gravity, wind, bouncy walls and more. Every mode keeps its own best score.

    Daily jar and missions

    A new daily jar every day, the same for every player, plus daily missions that earn stars. Stars unlock new themes for the jar.

    Jar trail puzzles

    24 puzzle jars, each with a goal and a limited number of throws. Finish in fewer throws to earn up to three stars.

    Collect every blob

    Each number is its own character. Bigger blobs grow eyebrows, a sprout, then a crown. Fill your collection and unlock achievements.
